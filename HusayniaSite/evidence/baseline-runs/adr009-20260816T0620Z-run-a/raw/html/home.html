MAJLIS-E-AZA OF ASHRA-E-MUHARRAM - Labbaik Ya Hussain (A.S.)

Dear Azadaar-e-Hussain (A.S.), The sacred days of mourning have arrived once again. As the call of "Ya Hussain (A.S.)" echoes in our hearts, we invite all lovers and Matamdars of Sayyed ush Shuhadaa (A.S.) to join us in the Majlis-e-Aza of Ashra-e-Muharram and pay their humble respects to Imam Hussain (A.S.) and the martyrs of Karbala. May Allah accept our mourning and devotion in these blessed days.

SHAHADAT HAZRAT MUSLIM BIN AQEEL AND MAJLISE TARHEEM - Marhooma Sister Neelam Khaki

With profound sorrow, we announce the Chehlum of Marḥūma Sister Neelam Khaki and commemorate the Shahadat of Muslim Bin Aqeel (A.S.). An Isāl-e-Sawāb gathering was held in her blessed memory. We extend our heartfelt condolences to her husband, Br. Mehboob Ali Khaki, her children, and all family members and loved ones. Mission Statement

Husaynia society is determined to go beyond rituals to strategic implementation of Imam Husayn’s message in the western society, as bearing the true message of Islam. We plan to build an interfaith community where people come together to share and discuss ideas and work on things they are passionate about. Recognizing we belong to one human race means we have a responsibility to act as a community to affirmatively address issues of social justice.
